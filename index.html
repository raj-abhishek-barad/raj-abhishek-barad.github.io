About

I work on autonomous planetary landing guidance and estimation, with an emphasis on fuel‑optimal descent under tight constraints (terrain clearance, bounded thrust, thrust lag, and navigation uncertainty). My current focus is on bridging closed‑form guidance structure with online replanning (receding/decreasing‑horizon MPC), and building simulation infrastructure for rigorous comparisons.

  • Free‑final‑time and constrained optimal control formulations for powered descent
  • Barrier / corridor constraints for terrain safety
  • Real‑time replanning and robustness under model mismatch and state‑estimation error
