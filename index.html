About

A little about me

I study how spacecraft can land themselves — accurately, safely, and with minimal fuel — under Dr. Satadal Ghosh at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. My research combines zero-effort-miss / zero-effort-velocity (ZEM/ZEV) guidance with velocity-aware barrier penalties and sliding-mode robustness, targeting the kind of terrain-aware powered descent you'd need for a Moon or Mars mission.

Alongside spacecraft GNC, I'm drawn to ground robotics — particularly safe motion planning and perception-aware navigation — and I enjoy building full-stack simulations in ROS 2 and Gazebo to close the loop between theory and hardware.

When I'm not debugging quaternion conventions or staring at phase portraits, I'm probably reading about control theory or thinking about how autonomous systems fail gracefully.
